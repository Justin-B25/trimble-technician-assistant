Station 4 — Battered Pile lab (sono tube)
  1. Stage 12×48 QUIK-TUBE at a known lean.
  2. Name rim shots {PileID}B1–B3 (bottom) and {PileID}T1–T3 (top), e.g. 1001B1 … 1001T3.
  3. Export CSV → drop below. Enter design cut-off N/E/Z and inclination to compare measured vs design.

Deliverables: cut-off coordinates (X = Easting, Y = Northing), batter angle (from vertical), azimuth (lean from North), and deviation ΔX / ΔY vs your design. The crosshair shows how far the measured cut-off is off North / East. If you enter a design Z, cut-off XY is the measured pile axis projected to that elevation.
