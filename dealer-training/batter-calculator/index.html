Drag a Siteworks point CSV here. Name points {PileID}B1–B3 (bottom rim) and {PileID}T1–T3 (top rim), e.g. 1001B1 … 1001T3. The calculator averages each ring to get bottom of pile center and top of pile center, then the vector between them as measured ΔN / ΔE / ΔZ (top − bottom).
