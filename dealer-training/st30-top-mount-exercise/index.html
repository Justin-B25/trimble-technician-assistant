    What the advertised spec means

    Optical TIP tilt error is a budget, not a mystery number: spec = TS + constant + (mm per °tilt × tilt°). This lab compares each tilt to your own 0° shot, so the total station (TS) is the same on every shot — leave TS at 0.

    0.2 m Top Mount

    XY: TS + 1 mm + 0.1 mm/° × tilt

    Z: TS + 1 mm + 0.1 mm/° × tilt

    1.6 m rod

    XY: TS + 2 mm + 0.3 mm/° × tilt

    Z: TS + 1 mm + 0.1 mm/° × tilt

    2.0 m rod

    XY: TS + 2.5 mm + 0.3 mm/° × tilt

    Z: TS + 1 mm + 0.1 mm/° × tilt
